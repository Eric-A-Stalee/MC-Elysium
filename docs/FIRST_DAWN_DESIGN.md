# The first dawn

Historical alpha 21–22 design checkpoint. Alpha 25 deliberately expands this
boundary into continuous Tartarus and physical surface ruptures; its current
rules and player route are in [Tartarus Unbound](UNBOUND.md). The no-invasion
constraint below belongs to the earlier, smaller chapter.

Elysium is a place worth keeping, not a disposable waiting room for a boss.
The surface remains a viable home. Its buried works explain both its beauty
and its arrested life. Exploration is voluntary; simply entering the realm,
reading an archive or returning the First Flame cannot start the clock.

This development run closes an entire arc instead of adding another unresolved
teaser. The player restores a craft, releases the arrested afternoon, learns
why the dawn cannot return, and chooses how to leave the realm.

## Playable checkpoints

1. **The smith's work.** The great hammer becomes a physical, supplied machine.
   Inputs are held in the works, interrupted jobs survive, output is collected
   in-world. Sunwrought metal makes useful expedition and harvest equipment.
   Its movement belongs to the existing forge, not a second generic crafting UI.
2. **The first sunset.** A deliberate first strike releases the celestial
   governor. Time belongs to Elysium alone and is saved, synchronized, and
   paused while the realm has no players. Night is a changed golden landscape,
   not the Nether: old watchfires and restrained cinder incursions become
   meaningful. The first dawn stalls until the old surface bindings answer.
3. **A world that can wake.** Three distinct watch traditions—harvest, water,
   and mountain—connect the biomes through small ruined landmarks. A forged
   instrument leads the expedition; repaired watchfires provide safe light and
   return a record of what was done. At the forge, the player can restore a
   living day/night cycle or deliberately bind another enduring golden hour.
   The choice remains revisitable through the repaired works.

## Principles

- No catastrophe from ordinary tourism; explain the release at its actual control.
- World state is shared, discoveries are personal, rewards cannot be duplicated
  by two simultaneous users, and a lost item must not make the story unwinnable.
- No offline deadline, block-destroying invasion, forced chunk loading, or
  arbitrary need to restart a world mid-chapter.
- Surface landmarks must work in ordinary daylight too. Darkness reveals their
  significance; it does not make buildings materialize from nowhere.
- Preserve the approved foliage, grass, terrain and Nordic settlement identity.
- Actual Minecraft captures and server tests determine what is finished.
  Concept imagery is direction, never evidence of an implemented feature.

All three parts are implemented in alpha 22. The [player guide](FIRST_DAWN.md)
describes the complete route; [validation notes](TESTING.md) record the tested
boundary. The alpha 21 working-forge checkpoint is preserved separately from
the alpha 22 first-dawn chapter. A larger Tartarus realm and later chapters
remain outside this release.
