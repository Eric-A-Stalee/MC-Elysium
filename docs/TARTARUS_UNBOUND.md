# Tartarus Unbound — evolving development direction

The alpha 22 first-dawn loop was too small and too neatly resolved. This branch
opens the world beneath its chamber and develops consequences that belong to
the scale of the myth. Checkpoints preserve useful experiments; they are places
to inspect the result and decide what to pursue next.

## The world beneath

Tartarus occupies the deep part of Elysium itself. The target is a continuous,
explorable world of dry ash wastes, hanging chain forests, hollow silt basins
and immense remains of imprisoned titans. The old Eye chamber is a threshold.
The first concept study suggested that imprisonment should shape the geography:
chains disappear into the roof, ribs read initially as cliffs, and old work
roads belong to machinery whose complete purpose is still obscure.

The terrain checkpoint extends the dimension to Y=-640, preserving the existing
surface formulas and adding a registered stratified generator. Its deep columns,
survey values and four biome regions share deterministic world coordinates.
The winding stair below the old chamber follows surveyed deep ground. This
checkpoint's geometry review uses explicit night vision; a separate Keeper's
Lamp view shows normal darkness. It is not a finished lighting treatment.

## The next questions to build into the world

Prometheus should be an encounter with someone who knows the cost of fire,
not a vending machine for the next key. Defeating the eagle creates space for
that encounter and his teaching. The first divine work should be materially
useful enough that choosing to forge matters, then release something that
changes how the familiar golden surface feels beneath a suspended moon.

Surface watches can shelter places and help people endure the consequences.
Their restoration cannot by itself erase what escaped. The underworld should
contain wardens as well as prisoners, and creatures whose behavior expresses
what they are: hunger that hunts, malice that seeks weakness, and machinery
that continues obeying a sentence no one living remembers.

The current next steps are inhabitants and Prometheus's prison, then the
relationship between divine forging, the vessel at the foundry, surface
ruptures, and the old deep bindings. Actual client captures and playable
encounters will determine the changes after each checkpoint. These paragraphs
describe direction, not a claim that every encounter is already implemented.

## The prison checkpoint

A dry, surveyed deep outcrop now carries a monumental embedded figure, fractured
bronze gantry, a narrow graded approach, and an open fighting ledge. The Bronze
Eagle alternates marked dives with real projectile volleys, then drops to a long
melee recovery. The voice in the restraint owns the encounter and remembers its
defeat. Prometheus teaches each visitor personally; persistent knowledge, not
the continued possession of a book, enables divine smithing. The Chain Resonator
changes its search to the prison below the deep threshold.

The concept study led to an architectural prisoner rather than a small humanoid
NPC. His body is built into the rock; the eagle is an articulated moving entity.
This needs actual client review before the silhouettes and material balance can
be considered settled. The book's warning anticipates the consequence systems
being developed next on this branch.
