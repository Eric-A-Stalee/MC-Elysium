# Tartarus Unbound

Elysium's golden surface now rests above a continuous underworld. The Eye's
chamber is a threshold, not the end of Tartarus. Its lower winding stair leads
into a cavern realm of ash wastes, hanging chains, black-water hollows and the
remains of titans. The dimension extends to Y=-640; most deep ground lies around
Y=-450 to -560. Start a fresh alpha world or fully regenerate Elysium.

## The expedition

1. Find a mountain archive, abandoned waystation or librarian's folio. Follow
   the survey to the buried Forge, repair its channels and recover a Chain
   Resonator. The coal-fed service fire still requires fuel and an open air supply.
2. Follow a Chain of Tartarus to its anchor, descend through the inspection
   passage, repair the counterweight crossing and confront the Cinderbound Eye.
   Its First Flame restores the Forge's full heat, but does not release the sky.
3. Continue down the winding stair beyond the Eye. Below Y=-248 the Chain
   Resonator surveys for **Prometheus's prison**. Bring food, blocks, a weapon
   and light. A placed Keeper's Lamp helps; darkness is intentional.
4. Defeat the Bronze Eagle. Its marked dive locks a landing position before
   moving; step away. Its bronze pinions are real projectiles and stop on stone.
   After attacking it drops into a long recovery where melee can reach it.
   Touch the glowing voice in the restraint to receive Prometheus's teaching.
   Knowledge belongs to each player and survives losing the book.
5. Return to the Forge. The Great Hammer Control's book explains all material
   bills. **Completing the first three-stroke batch opens the sealed vessel and
   releases the sun.** Loading, pausing, an obstructed stroke or returning the
   Flame alone does not. The control warns before this choice.

In Peaceful, the prison's voice offers the lesson without combat. The surface
may remain peaceful indefinitely if no one completes the first divine work.

## What escapes

The sun descends from tick 11,500. Night begins at 14,000; the moon eventually
stops at **18,000**. The realm clock and rupture pressure pause when Elysium has
no players. The Overworld clock is independent.

Stray Cinders still haunt dark surface ground. The new **Ashen Hungers** are
blind: walking and sprinting carry sound, crouching is quieter, and solid cover
blocks direct detection. They pursue the last sound instead of tracking a
silent player through walls. A forged **Hush Charm**, held in the off hand,
reduces walking noise; it does not make sprinting silent. Taking a hit alerts
nearby members of the pack. Watch sanctuaries and placed Ward Lamps protect
against their attacks as well as the Sentenced's chains.

Unrepaired bindings build pressure during occupied nights. A surface rupture
begins away from a player with four seconds of ash and rumbling. It then tears
open a small irregular crater and releases hunters; some wounds also release
a **Sentenced** chain warden. Sorrow wounds can release **Mire Mourners**, whose
false hearths mark the ground before a damaging lament. Real light, Hope and
solid cover shelter against them. The warning is real: standing beside the wound
when it opens hurts and throws the player. Wardens telegraph a locked chain
lane, then leave a long recovery. Sidestep or use solid cover.

Wounds choose loaded natural ground outside sanctuaries. They do not force-load
chunks or excavate inventories, and every terrain write is rechecked after the
warning. Both mob spawning and mob griefing must be enabled for rupture events;
Peaceful suppresses them. Continued forging adds pressure, within a bounded
frequency and local encounter cap. A loaded open wound can release more hunters
until repaired. The three surface watches create refuges, not a cure for Tartarus.

## The deep restraints

Take **Hope's Ember** from the opened vessel beside the Forge's rear shrine.
If it is lost, an Elysium Fragment can kindle a replacement there. Forge
**Binding Nails**. After Prometheus has taught you and the vessel is open,
normal use of the Chain Resonator below the Eye seeks the next unrepaired
restraint. Sneak-use retains its remembered Forge bearing.

| Restraint | How to quiet it | Finish |
|---|---|---|
| Hunger's Maw | Offer eight wheat to the restraint. Its hunger pauses for twelve seconds. | Use one Binding Nail during the opening. |
| Sorrow's Cistern | Hold Hope in either hand, crouch within four blocks and remain still in actual block light of at least level eight for four seconds. Bring or place a lamp. | Switch to a Binding Nail within ten seconds; Hope is retained. |
| Dominion's Captive Throne | Shelter from the Crownless's command and defeat his restraint's own Sentenced jailer. Its defeat persists through saving. | Use one Binding Nail. In Peaceful, examine the restraint first. |

A Binding Nail can also close an individual open surface wound. Restoring a deep
restraint prevents new wounds of that kind and quiets its existing loaded wounds.
**Scars remain. Escaped Hungers, Sentenced and Mourners remain.** Restoring the sky does
not silently undo the world's history. Repairs and watch progress are shared;
Prometheus teaches individual players.

Examine each restraint with an empty hand to copy its three-page testimony; a blank book makes a replacement.

The ruined places are physically generated structures. Their approaches follow
surveyed terrain, rather than relying on runtime room excavation. The Maw's
broken jaw, the Cistern's water and mourner, and the living Crownless tell different
parts of the imprisonment. Prometheus's immense body is built into the rock;
his voice is an interactive block, and the eagle is an animated entity.

Dominion now holds the **Crownless**, a living sovereign roughly 34 blocks high
while seated. Before divine smithing, he sleeps. The First Flame lets his voice
escape without freeing his wrists. A drawn breath warns of the command that
travels across the ground; broken masonry stops it. The command hurts exposed
survivors and directs nearby Hungers and Sentenced toward them. Repair the
restraint to quiet him. Ordinary weapons cannot kill this captive.

The restraints are not three interchangeable treasure rooms. Hunger is a need
that outlived its body; Sorrow is grief that offers counterfeit comfort; Dominion
is a will that can command others even while chained. Their remains, inhabitants
and repairs express those differences. Tiny Ash Moths offer a quieter contrast:
they gather around genuine emitting blocks and ignore a Mourner's false glow.
The [inhabitants guide](INHABITANTS.md) covers the encounters, art and current limits.

## Useful work

The in-game Smith's Charge is generated from the same recipe catalog as the
press. These recipes extend the existing Sunwrought tools and watch instruments.

| Selector | Complete bill | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Feather | 1 Sunwrought Ingot, 4 copper ingots, 1 feather | Bronze Pinion |
| Bronze Pinion | 2 Sunwrought Ingots, 1 Bronze Pinion, 2 Cinder Glass | 4 Binding Nails |
| Ashen Fang | 2 Sunwrought Ingots, 6 Ashen Fangs | Hush Charm |

Prometheus's initial gift starts the repair supply; tempered pinions make it
renewable. Ember roots sometimes yield Cinder Glass. Hungers supply fangs and
sometimes glass. The old Sunwrought glass recipe remains available after peace.

## A world that can wake

Repair **all three surface watch traditions and all three deep restraints**.
Restore heat to the Forge's rear governor. Sneak-use a clock there to release a
living day/night cycle, or an Elysium Fragment to choose the enduring golden
hour. Neither instrument is consumed. The choice can be revisited. The repaired
world retains its scars and escaped creatures; the underworld remains explorable.

`/elysium story` reports watch and restraint progress without cheats.
For inspection with commands enabled, use these inside Elysium:

```
/locate structure elysium:prometheus_prison
/locate structure elysium:hunger_binding
/locate structure elysium:sorrow_binding
/locate structure elysium:dominion_binding
/locate biome elysium:ash_wastes
/locate biome elysium:chain_forest
/locate biome elysium:hollow_mire
/locate biome elysium:titan_grave
```

The resonator's bounded survival survey uses actual starts. If it finds no site,
move to another region and survey again. It reports the site's depth as well as
its direction and horizontal distance. See [testing notes](TESTING.md) for the
exact evidence and remaining playtesting limits.
