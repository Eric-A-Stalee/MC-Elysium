# The first sunset and the first dawn

This optional chapter begins after returning the First Flame. The sky remains
at 11,500 until you deliberately complete the first divine batch at the **Great Hammer Control**. Prometheus must first teach you the craft. Examining the works, loading materials, and interrupted strokes do not open the vessel. The control warns before starting. See [Tartarus Unbound](UNBOUND.md) for the expanded route and consequences.

## Becoming the smith

Read the **Smith's Charge** at the control. Offer a blank book for another copy.
Load a recipe by using its selector item against the control. The complete
material bill is taken together and held in the machine. Three physical hammer
strokes produce one batch; use the control to collect it. Sneak-use an empty
hand again to pause. Loss of full heat pauses the job too. Blocked or damaged
head geometry stops movement without clearing player blocks. Work, head offset
and finished output survive saving. Mining the control drops its stored items.

| Select with | Complete material bill | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Iron ingot | 3 iron, 2 copper, 1 gold | 4 Sunwrought Ingots |
| Amethyst shard | 2 Sunwrought Ingots, 1 amethyst | Watchfire Seal |
| Compass | 2 Sunwrought Ingots, 1 compass, 2 amethyst | Dawn Wayfinder |
| Iron hoe | 2 Sunwrought Ingots, 1 iron hoe | Harvest Sickle |
| Glass | 1 Sunwrought Ingot, 4 glass | 4 Ember Glass |
| Keeper's Lamp | 2 Sunwrought Ingots, 1 Keeper's Lamp, 1 amethyst | Ward Lamp |

The sickle selectively harvests ripe crops in a 3×3 patch, using ordinary break
hooks and loot. Seeds already in your inventory pay for replanting vanilla
crops. It has 768 durability, spending one per plant. A Ward Lamp supplies
level 12 held light throughout Elysium and level 15 placed light; it repels
stray cinders while carried and shelters a 12-block radius when placed.

## The unremembered night

The sun reaches night after about two minutes, then stalls at tick 18,000
without a dawn. There is no deadline: Elysium's clock pauses when the realm
has no players. The Overworld clock is independent. The first night cannot
be skipped by sleeping. Three different watches and three deep restraints need repair.

Use a **Dawn Wayfinder** to survey for the next unanswered tradition. Its
search is bounded and uses real structure starts, like the existing folio.
If a region has no result, move to another region and use it again. Its bearing
gives direction and approximate distance. Sneak-use points to the awakened
Forge. Once all three watches answer, normal use points home too.

| Watch | Landscape | Offer with one Watchfire Seal |
|---|---|---|
| Harvest Watch | Golden fields and birch woods | 8 wheat |
| Water Watch | Watermeadows and amber lake regions | A water bucket; the bucket returns |
| High Watch | Highlands and Ivory Peaks | 4 amethyst shards |

The ruins exist before the night. Individual footings follow the ground;
only the small central socket has a level platform. Their records tell three
parts of the old repair. Night reveals a weak ember; a restored watch becomes
a bright, permanent 48-block sanctuary. Restoring several of the same kind
does not substitute for another tradition. Progress is shared by the world.

**Stray Cinders** appear occasionally on loaded, dark surface ground during
the unresolved night. They visibly open before a short damaging rush. They
cannot burn or break terrain, do not appear in Peaceful or when mob spawning
is disabled, and disappear when the crisis is resolved. Their Cinder Glass
can be combined with four ordinary glass blocks to make four **Ember Glass**:
a warm translucent building light at level 10. The great hammer can also temper
four glass with one Sunwrought Ingot into four Ember Glass, so this building
material remains renewable after the night is resolved.

## Choosing the sky

Repair the missing runner to the Forge's rear shrine and keep its restored
heat supplied. At the **Celestial Governor**, after all three watches answer
and Hunger, Sorrow and Dominion are bound again:

- Sneak-use a **clock** to release the living cycle. The first dawn approaches,
  then ordinary days and nights continue. Sleeping can advance this repaired sky.
- Sneak-use an **Elysium Fragment** to bind an enduring golden hour again.
  This is a completed resolution too; the repaired watches remain lit.

Neither instrument is consumed. The working governor lets you revisit the
choice. Surface ruptures can change natural terrain during the unresolved
night; occupied play adds pressure, and an empty realm has no offline deadline.
`/elysium story` reports the chapter and next objective without requiring cheats.

Use fresh alpha worlds or regenerate Elysium for the new machinery, governor
and watch ruins. This chapter makes no saved-world migration promise.
